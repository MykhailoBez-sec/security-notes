# Day 6. Conclusions on job openings

## Conclusions

### 1. Core requirements (appear in most job posts)
- SIEM, networking & firewalls/VPN, EDR/XDR
- MITRE ATT&CK / Kill Chain, alert triage, ticketing & escalation (SOP, SLA, ITIL)
- Windows/Linux, Active Directory, basic scripting (Python/PowerShell)
- English C1 is the working language in international companies

### 2. Gaps in my roadmap
- Microsoft stack: Sentinel, KQL, Defender, SC-200
- Splunk
- Hands-on EDR and firewall/VPN practice
- SOAR and ITIL basics (concepts are enough)

### 3. Real filters vs wishlists
- Real filters: 1–3 years of IT/SOC/NOC experience, work authorization,
  shift work 24/7
- Wishlists: advanced certs (GCIH, GCIA, OSCP, SC-100/300/401),
  "3+ years" in roles titled Tier 1
- Security+ is a strong plus in about half of the posts

### 4. Search strategy
- Target English-first international companies: MSSPs, product companies
- Skip posts that require a local language
- Common entry path: IT support / NOC → SOC L1
